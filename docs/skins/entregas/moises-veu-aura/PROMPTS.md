### Estilo 1

Edit the approved full-body Jornada Bíblica RPG sprite of the outfit inspired by Moses in Exodus 34:29–35. This is a narrowly scoped character-art edit. Preserve the approved semirealistic painterly 2.5D texture, identity, complexion, hairstyle, broad mantle, exact panel colors and embroidery, waist sash, two stone tablets, grip of both hands, stance, visibility of both sandals and all full-body proportions. Add one visible, modest face veil of ancient fine warm ivory flax. The veil fastens at the brow and near both temples in the existing hair or wrap and hangs in front of the entire face from above the eyes past the chin. Cover the eyes, nose, mouth and chin with a continuous curtain of naturally draped linen, with credible narrow vertical folds and a subtly woven hem. Facial shape is only suggested through the fabric and diffused light. Read as a cloth suspended in front of the face, with no eye holes, mask edge, opening or bare strip of face. It visibly shines through the fine veil into the weave and lights the near edges of the cloth. Make it bright enough to read in a small game portrait. Soft oval feathered light, seamless transparent edges, no hard ring, drawn disk, iconography, particles, flames, rays, cast floor light or separate objects. Keep the aura near the head and stay within the same 1024 × 1536 canvas. Genuine RGBA with empty transparency elsewhere. Preserve the two hands, stone tablets, tunic and all pixels of the costume below the shoulder as closely as possible.
